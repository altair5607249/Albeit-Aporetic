# Crusaders —— Writing
This contains my literary work on "The Ashes", including, but not limited to: "Albeit Aporetic", a science-fantasy chronicle cycle that my friends and I colloquially call "The Novel", which this repo is mainly about; "The CC", a failed iteration of "The Novel"; "CC Lore", another failed iteration; and lore anecdotes.

## Structure
- "Albeit-Aporetic/" —— current iteration of the written narrative of "The Ashes"
- "Drafts/" —— earlier iterations, including some rough material, kept public for anyone curious about the process itself

## Note
During COVID-19, I grew bored sitting in my room all day with minimal activities to engage in. Sure, it wasn't too different from my daily life at the time, since I usually occupied myself with video games 24/7, but at least without the pandemic there was room for me to be forced outside. Therefore, I sought ways to reinvent fun for myself. 

One such way was creative writing, and the novelty of it never wore off even after the pandemic passed over. However, suffice to say that there were many things I didn't understand during the beginning stages of my literary journey, only that it was fun to let my ideas fly freely on the pages, whether physical or digital. 

That's why you, the viewer, may stumble across my written shenanigans while browsing this repository (and frankly any of my repos that display my earlier creative works). Therein, I suggest that you take them with as little seriousness as possible (or don't, I don't really mind)!

Anyways, thanks for reading, and happy building!
